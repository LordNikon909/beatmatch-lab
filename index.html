Load a record into each deck to check harmonic compatibility.
Idle. Press Play on a deck to start the set, load a record into the other deck, then press Beatmatch Mix.

Beatmatch Mix always mixes whichever deck is loaded-but-silent into whichever deck is currently live — if both or neither are playing, it stays disabled until exactly one is. Assumes 4/4 time and that a record's own MIDI start is its downbeat. Phrase detection is a self-similarity heuristic, not verified song structure. Key detection correlates a duration-weighted pitch-class histogram against the 24 major/minor key profiles (Krumhansl-Schmuckler) over the whole record — solid for one stable key, blind to mid-song key changes, unreliable on drum-only or heavily chromatic material. Every channel — melodic tracks on their own declared GM instrument, percussion on the GM drum kit — plays through a real SoundFont2 synthesizer (SpessaSynth, GeneralUser GS bank), so drop in a different .sf2 to change the whole instrument palette.
